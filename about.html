Who We Are

A Boutique Built On Elegance & Trust

Pinklady Enterprise is a leading Nigerian fashion & lifestyle enterprise based in Kano State. What began as a small boutique has grown into a trusted name across Northern Nigeria, known for curating luxury abayas, kaftans, handbags, shoes, jewelry, and perfumes that speak to modern African women.

Every piece on our racks is chosen with intention — quality fabrics, refined craftsmanship, and designs that honor Northern Nigerian culture while embracing global fashion sensibilities.

Our Mission

To make premium, culturally-rooted fashion accessible to every woman who walks through our doors — online or in-store.

Our Vision

To become Nigeria's most trusted luxury fashion & lifestyle brand, celebrated for elegance and authenticity.

Our Core Values

Quality, integrity, cultural pride, and customer satisfaction guide every decision we make.

A Message From Our CEO

"Style Is A Way To Say Who You Are"

At Pinklady Enterprise, we believe fashion is more than clothing — it's a form of self-expression. Since founding this boutique, my goal has always been to create a space where every woman feels celebrated, confident, and beautiful. Thank you for trusting us to be part of your style journey.

Fatima Aminu Chief Executive Officer
